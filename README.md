## About Me

I am a biomedical engineer working at the intersection of **medical imaging, artificial intelligence, computational modeling, wearable sensing, patient-specific medical design, and medical-device development**.

I earned my **M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Arizona State University (GPA: 3.65/4.00)** and my **B.Tech. in Biomedical Engineering from Adamas University (CGPA: 8.88/10)**. My experience ranges from CT/MRI-based anatomical reconstruction and implant design to chest X-ray deep learning, finite-element tissue modeling, physiological signal processing, and regulatory analysis.

📍 Tempe, Arizona · Interested in biomedical engineering, medical imaging, research, AI, and medical-device roles.
